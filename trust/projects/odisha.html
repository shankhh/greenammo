Transforming rural sanitation and energy access in 11 remote districts through eco-toilets and solar solutions.

Rural Sanitation & Energy Transformation

Odisha

  • Collaborative Rural Development – Partnered with Milaap and Gram Utthan to reach 11 remote districts lacking basic electricity and sanitation.
  • Eco-Toilet Construction Using Local Resources – Designed and built durable mud toilets using abundant red soil, providing a low-cost, culturally compatible solution for rural sanitation.
  • Renewable Energy for Health & Safety – Introduced solar lamps as sustainable alternatives to kerosene and disposable battery lamps, reducing indoor air pollution, mitigating fire hazards, and improving household safety.
