Welcome to GreenAmmo,
Where Impact Meets Innovation

Explore Our Initiatives

GreenAmmo is a Trust dedicated to environmental protection, sustainable development, community empowerment, and infrastructure innovation. We venture into vulnerable ecological and geographical landscapes—from mountain ranges to coastal ghats—to establish self-sustaining waste management systems. By empowering locals to lead these initiatives, we ensure lasting environmental resilience. Nature knows no boundaries, and neither do we. Waste knows no domain, and neither do we.
